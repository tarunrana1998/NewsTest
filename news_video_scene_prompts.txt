INDIA NEWS TODAY | SCENE-BY-SCENE AI VIDEO PROMPTS
Companion to news_script_hinglish.txt
Format: 16:9 landscape, 1080p, editorial YouTube news package
Visual approach: contemporary Indian newsroom, crisp documentary-style b-roll,
subtle dry humor in transitions, neutral colors, natural movement, legible pacing.

HOW TO USE
Generate each scene as a separate clip using its VIDEO PROMPT. Keep the same
presenter/avatar, wardrobe, color grade, and music bed across clips if your tool
supports continuity. Add the supplied ON-SCREEN TEXT in your video editor rather
than asking the video model to render lettering. Lay the supplied VOICEOVER over
the clip, trimming or extending b-roll to match the narration. Durations below
are approximate; the full voiceover may need longer than a single generated clip.

GLOBAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR EVERY SCENE
Create original editorial news visuals, not a recreation of footage from any
publisher or broadcaster. Use a calm, credible Indian news presenter in a simple
modern studio only for the intro and outro; use illustrative b-roll for reports.
Natural expressions and gestures, respectful representation, clean composition,
subtle camera motion, realistic lighting, no sensationalism. Avoid logos, watermarks,
fake newspaper front pages, fake official documents, fabricated quotes, and
AI-generated text. Do not depict a real named person or imply that illustrative
footage is authentic evidence. Use only the provided captions in post-production.

NEGATIVE PROMPT
No political party logos or campaign symbols, no fabricated police bodycam or
detention footage, no identifiable real people, no explosion or attack reenactment,
no injury imagery, no panic, no stereotypes, no distorted hands or faces, no
garbled lettering, no source logos, no watermark, no dramatic breaking-news
sirens, no misleading map borders, no fake evidence.

SCENE 1 | OPENING MONTAGE
Approximate duration: 8-12 seconds
Purpose: Hook the viewer and preview the four stories.

VIDEO PROMPT
Create a fast but composed opening montage for an Indian current-affairs YouTube
bulletin. Start with a clean digital map of India made from neutral lines, then
quickly cut through four symbolic shots: a long paper queue becoming shorter,
a neatly arranged set of everyday clothing, a quiet Rajasthan street with a
protest date on a generic calendar shown without readable text, and an Indian
traveller checking a phone indoors in a calm Gulf-region apartment. End on a
confident Indian presenter in a contemporary compact newsroom, looking into
camera. Editorial, informed, lightly witty pacing, not sensational. Leave clear
negative space for captions. No readable text generated in the video.

VOICEOVER
Aaj ki headlines mein Haryana ke pending cases kam hue, Kerala High Court ne
kapdon par choice clear ki, Rajasthan se ek detention ka allegation aaya, aur
Saudi Arabia mein Indians ke liye safety advisory jaari hui. Matlab news cycle
ne aaj bhi calendar ko weekend samajhne se mana kar diya.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
INDIA NEWS TODAY
Haryana | Kerala | Rajasthan | Saudi Arabia

ON-SCREEN TEXT
FACTS, CLAIMS, CONTEXT

SCENE 3 | HARYANA SIR HEARINGS
Approximate duration: 25-35 seconds

VIDEO PROMPT
Create illustrative administrative b-roll in Haryana, India: a quiet public
service office waiting area, people seated at a respectful distance, staff
sorting plain unmarked paper files, then a simple abstract queue graphic with
many dots reducing to fewer dots. Use a neutral government-office setting with
no visible seals, personal data, readable forms, real official branding, or
identifiable citizens. End with a wide shot of an orderly corridor. Calm,
fact-focused tone; a tiny visual hint of the queue shortening can carry the
script's dry humor without mocking people waiting for hearings.

VOICEOVER
Haryana ke Chief Electoral Officer ne pending Special Intensive Revision,
yaani SIR notices par teen din ka drive chalaya. Is dauraan 57,339 pending
hearing cases dispose kiye gaye. Ab bhi 2,85,834 cases hearing ka wait kar rahe
 hain. Teen din mein itne cases clear hona ek significant update hai. Lekin
 2.85 lakh log ab bhi queue mein hain, toh kaam khatam nahi, bas queue thodi chhoti hui
hai. Sarkari process mein “pending” shabd ka stamina kabhi underestimate mat
kijiye.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
HARYANA SIR HEARINGS
57,339 cases disposed
2,85,834 still awaiting hearings
Source: The Hindu

SCENE 4 | KERALA HIGH COURT: ATTIRE AND CHOICE
Approximate duration: 25-35 seconds

VIDEO PROMPT
Create respectful, non-literal editorial visuals about personal clothing
choice and access to a workplace. Show a woman educator from behind choosing
between a sari and a churidar-kameez in a simple wardrobe scene, then walking
through a bright school corridor. Cut to a symbolic courthouse exterior in
Kerala with no readable sign, no judge, no courtroom reenactment, and no real
person likeness. Add a gentle visual gag: a clothing hanger beside a tiny
imaginary stack of law books, understated and tasteful. Warm daylight, dignified
and non-mocking. Leave clear space for editor captions.

VOICEOVER
Kerala High Court ne kaha ki attire, yaani kapdon ka chunav, ek citizen ki
personal choice hai. Case Kollam ke ek school headmistress ki petition se juda
tha. Unka allegation tha ki churidar-kameez pehenne par school manager ne unhe
entry nahi di, jabki woh sari ke bajay ye outfit pehen kar aayi thi. Court ka
point simple hai: kapde pehenne wale ke, choice bhi pehenne wale ki. Kapdon par
dress code debate kabhi-kabhi itni lambi chalti hai ki lagta hai wardrobe ko bhi
apna legal counsel rakhna padega.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
KERALA HIGH COURT
“Attire is a citizen’s choice”
Source: The Hindu

SCENE 5 | RAJASTHAN: REPORTED DETENTION CLAIM
Approximate duration: 20-30 seconds

VIDEO PROMPT
Create a restrained, clearly illustrative scene for a developing news report:
a generic Rajasthan city street in daylight, a public noticeboard with blank
paper, and a calendar page with an unmarked date shape. Use an empty street or
wide public space only; do not show a person being stopped, arrested, detained,
questioned, or placed in a vehicle. Do not show police insignia, identifiable
officers, the named spokesperson, or an event presented as verified. Shift to
a neutral abstract graphic of a speech bubble beside a question mark, signaling
that this is a reported claim with details still unclear. Serious, measured
visual tone, no dramatic shadows or sirens.

VOICEOVER
Citizens for Justice and Peace, yaani CJP, ne claim kiya hai ki unke
spokesperson ko Rajasthan Police ne pick up kiya. CJP ke mutabik, yeh October 10
ko planned protest se pehle hua. Abhi available report mein detention ki wajah
par poori clarity nahi hai, isliye isse allegation ke roop mein hi report karna
zaroori hai. Yahan facts se aage jaana theek nahi hoga. Aage ki details saamne
aayengi toh picture clearer hogi; tab tak headline ko headline hi rehne dete
hain, courtroom ka verdict nahi banate.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
REPORTED CLAIM | DETAILS UNCLEAR
CJP alleges spokesperson was picked up
Source: The Indian Express

SCENE 6 | SAUDI ARABIA SAFETY ADVISORY
Approximate duration: 20-30 seconds

VIDEO PROMPT
Create calm public-safety visuals, not conflict footage. Show an Indian
traveller in a quiet apartment in Saudi Arabia checking a phone notification
with no readable text, closing curtains, and contacting family by video call.
Cut to a generic map route between India and Saudi Arabia using simple lines
without disputed borders or military markings. Do not depict an attack, weapon,
explosion, injured people, panic, or the advisory as an official document.
Comforting natural light and steady camera. Finish on the traveller safely
indoors, attentive to their phone.

VOICEOVER
Saudi Arabia mein Indian Embassy ne citizens ko indoors rehne ki advisory di.
Yeh advisory region mein Houthi attack ke baad aayi hai. Saudi Arabia mein reh
rahe Indians ko local authorities ke instructions follow karne chahiye aur
embassy ki updates par nazar rakhni chahiye. Is story mein punchline se zyada
zaroori precaution hai. Agar aap ya aapke family members wahan hain, official
advisory ko priority dijiye aur safe rahiye.

ON-SCREEN TEXT
SAFETY ADVISORY
Indians in Saudi Arabia: follow official updates
Source: The Indian Express

SCENE 7 | OUTRO AND SOURCES
Approximate duration: 12-18 seconds

VIDEO PROMPT
Return to the same presenter and newsroom from Scene 2 for visual continuity.
Presenter gives a warm, concise sign-off. On the background display, show four
clean generic source-link bars or neutral publication-name placeholders to be
added in editing; do not generate logos or text. End with a simple fade to a
dark neutral background and clear empty space for the editor to add subscribe
and source-link graphics. Friendly, responsible, understated ending.

VOICEOVER
Toh dosto, aaj ki updates mein process, personal choice, ek reported allegation
aur ek important safety advisory shamil thi. Sources ke links description mein
hain, khud bhi check kijiye, aur kisi bhi developing story par nayi verified
information ka wait kijiye. Video useful laga ho toh like aur subscribe karein.
Milte hain next update mein. Tab tak informed rahiye, safe rahiye, aur headlines
ko forward karne se pehle ek baar source zaroor dekh lijiye. Namaskar!
